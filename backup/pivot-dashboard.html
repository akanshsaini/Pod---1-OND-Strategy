Calculations and source coverage

Match rate = total responses served ÷ total Ad Exchange requests. eCPM = total revenue ÷ total impressions × 1,000. Request eCPM = total revenue ÷ total requests × 1,000. Delivery rate = impressions ÷ responses served. Zero denominators and missing observations display n.a.; observed zero match rate displays 0.00%.

Revenue is USD as specified in your GAM report. Active View viewability cannot be correctly rolled up from this export because measurable-impression counts are absent. Blank domains, mobile-app labels and summary rows are excluded from website analysis. No pricing-rule or floor-change recommendation is inferred from these metrics.
