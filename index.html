Metric definitions & report coverage

Aggregated match rate and eCPM use summed counts and revenue. Source rows preserve the rates reported by GAM, so rounded source revenue can produce small differences in recalculated eCPM. Missing rows and zero denominators display n.a.

Active View viewability requires viewable and measurable impression counts for aggregation. Your files supply only its percentage: that original value is included in Source rows. Import those two additional counts to enable weighted viewability across dates, websites and devices.

Website pageviews are full-site totals for matching dates. They are available when grouping by website, optionally date or network, and are never repeated across country/device breakdowns. GAM geo/device filters do not change full-site pageviews.
